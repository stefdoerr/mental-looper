MENTAL LOOPER

The final face. One thing left to pick: the on/off light top left, in place of the little head. Four options are below the box. Click one to put it on the box, and click the light on the box to switch the plugin off (the big head goes X-eyed).

New: waiting to record. Click the mouth. With Auto Rec or Sync on, a REC press first waits (ARM or WAIT on the MOD display). The face shows it: the mouth opens halfway and trembles, the brows go up. Then the take starts and it screams. In this demo the wait lasts 1.5 s.
